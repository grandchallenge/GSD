# GSD — Generalization-State Dynamics

Grand Challenge Labs research programme for studying non-monotone generalization states during model training.

This repository is the canonical execution and evidence surface for GSD.

Scope includes:

- behavioural transition discovery and adversarial validation;
- checkpoint trajectory measurement;
- recovery/accessibility tests;
- CPS optimizer-state diagnostics;
- K-DIAGNOSTICS/operator localization;
- curriculum and data-control experiments;
- optimizer and architecture stabilization studies;
- scale-up and downstream confirmation.

GSD is GCL-wide. It is not governed by, or operationally coupled to, the Adaptive Intelligence Atlas. Atlas, standards, Residual, CPS, K-DIAGNOSTICS, optimizer, architecture, and curriculum programmes may consume accepted GSD results independently.

Claim firewall:

- behavioural change does not establish mechanism change;
- recovery does not establish literal mechanism identity;
- no universal scalar diagnostic is assumed;
- small-model results do not silently promote to larger scales;
- capacity allocation remains a hypothesis unless independently supported.
